# Tenant Isolation Policy: Customer Relationship Management

## A. Rule
Every CRM command, query, event, replay cursor, dashboard query, and export carries tenant_id and principal_id. No Customer Relationship Management bounded context may infer tenant scope from hostname, session cookie, marketplace settlement reference, or source-system identifier alone.

## B. Isolation evidence
- account-master: API, worker, catalog, SLO, dashboard, Cedar, and audit evidence require tenant_id equality before mutation or disclosure.
- opportunity: API, worker, catalog, SLO, dashboard, Cedar, and audit evidence require tenant_id equality before mutation or disclosure.
- quote: API, worker, catalog, SLO, dashboard, Cedar, and audit evidence require tenant_id equality before mutation or disclosure.
- service-case: API, worker, catalog, SLO, dashboard, Cedar, and audit evidence require tenant_id equality before mutation or disclosure.
- campaign: API, worker, catalog, SLO, dashboard, Cedar, and audit evidence require tenant_id equality before mutation or disclosure.
- loyalty-ledger: API, worker, catalog, SLO, dashboard, Cedar, and audit evidence require tenant_id equality before mutation or disclosure.

## C. Marketplace boundary
Marketplace settles tenant deals per ADR-0314. Customer Relationship Management stores settlement references only after tenant isolation has already been proven by policy, audit, and source-system checks.
