# Warehouse

## Purpose
Warehouse is the SAP EWM parity microservice for Inbound Processing, Outbound Processing, Slotting, Yard Management, Labor Management. It keeps a flat per-microservice layout, avoids ERP platform ownership, and composes with workflow-engine, ontology, policy, marketplace, observability, and regional-pack services.

## Bounded contexts
- inbound-delivery: tenant-scoped command, query, event, replay, and audit surface.
- outbound-delivery: tenant-scoped command, query, event, replay, and audit surface.
- putaway-task: tenant-scoped command, query, event, replay, and audit surface.
- picking-wave: tenant-scoped command, query, event, replay, and audit surface.
- yard-appointment: tenant-scoped command, query, event, replay, and audit surface.
- labor-assignment: tenant-scoped command, query, event, replay, and audit surface.

## Contracts
- REST: contracts/openapi-v1.yaml, OpenAPI 3.2.0.
- Events: contracts/asyncapi-v1.yaml, AsyncAPI 3.1.0.
- gRPC: contracts/warehouse-v1.proto, proto3.
- Naming: BNF v4.1 and ADR-0105 layers api, rest, application, usecase, domain, kernel, adapter, worker, governance.

## Operating posture
HTTP/3 is the default edge transport, ECH is advertised on tenant ingress, PQC hybrid negotiation is offered where peers support it, and fallback order is HTTP/3, HTTP/2, then HTTP/1.1. Marketplace settles tenant deals per ADR-0314.
