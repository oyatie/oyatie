# Quality Management

## Purpose
Quality Management is the SAP QM parity microservice for Inspection Plans, Certificates of Analysis, Quality Notifications, Audit Management. It keeps a flat per-microservice layout, avoids ERP platform ownership, and composes with workflow-engine, ontology, policy, marketplace, observability, and regional-pack services.

## Bounded contexts
- inspection-plan: tenant-scoped command, query, event, replay, and audit surface.
- inspection-lot: tenant-scoped command, query, event, replay, and audit surface.
- certificate-of-analysis: tenant-scoped command, query, event, replay, and audit surface.
- quality-notification: tenant-scoped command, query, event, replay, and audit surface.
- quality-hold: tenant-scoped command, query, event, replay, and audit surface.
- audit-evidence: tenant-scoped command, query, event, replay, and audit surface.

## Contracts
- REST: contracts/openapi-v1.yaml, OpenAPI 3.2.0.
- Events: contracts/asyncapi-v1.yaml, AsyncAPI 3.1.0.
- gRPC: contracts/quality-management-v1.proto, proto3.
- Naming: BNF v4.1 and ADR-0105 layers api, rest, application, usecase, domain, kernel, adapter, worker, governance.

## Operating posture
HTTP/3 is the default edge transport, ECH is advertised on tenant ingress, PQC hybrid negotiation is offered where peers support it, and fallback order is HTTP/3, HTTP/2, then HTTP/1.1. Marketplace settles tenant deals per ADR-0314.
