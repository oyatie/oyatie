## RALPLAN-DR Summary

### Principles

1. **Authority before activity, no vacuum.** A transition replaces an incumbent only after the successor is authoritative and proven. Accepted ADRs and the incumbent required admission context (today `oya-ci-required`, itself debrand-scope and transition-bound) stay binding until an owned debranded successor exists.
2. **Fail-closed everywhere.** Unverifiable attestation, unprovenanced numerics, uncataloged scanner hits, unclassified census identities, out-of-hot-set landings, unbound observation receipts, absent scheduled gate runs, and any tracker divergence all block. Green admission never authorizes a landing by itself; liveness, deploy counts, a single green run, or commit volume never close an exit.
3. **Disposition precedes relocation.** Every legacy target gets an explicit forever-shape disposition (refactor/rewrite/remove/reconsider/replan/justified relocation). `git mv` is not a disposition. Trees already under apparent destination names are not presumed compliant; whole-tree proof including nested files is required.
4. **Every ratified number carries a corridor.** No threshold is ratifiable without a measured live-tree or live-workload baseline plus explicit margin, benchmarked against a public hyperscaler parity class. This plan therefore contains no thresholds and defers every numeric slot to freeze.
5. **Destination identity is post-reorg capability identity.** No exit criterion, deliverable, work item, or evidence anchor binds `cloud-*`, `cloud_`, `oya-*`, `oya_`, `oya/**`, or `cloud/**` as destination identity. Those names appear only as target-set scope, disposition inventory, or bounded transition bridges with a recorded expiry.
6. **Observation is not authority.** An immutable content-hashed measurement or enumeration receipt that asserts no status and mints no work item is not authority-bearing. It may be produced before its canonical id exists and must bind to one before freeze; binding is a gate and an unbound receipt is inadmissible to the corridor.
7. **A mechanism must exist before the moment it is claimed to govern.** No evidence contract in this plan asserts a gate run, a refusal, or a governing artifact that did not exist at the time claimed. Where a prohibition binds normatively before its enforcement mechanism goes live, the interval is named, bounded, and closed by an audited retrospective proof — never by an implied run.

### Global Binding Rules (every wave)

1. **No-new-reorg-target-debt: normatively bound at T2, mechanically enforced from T3b, interval audited** (CRIT-2). From bootstrap T2 onward no wave, lane, or bootstrap step may create new code, capability, crate, module, path dependency, exit dependency, work item, or evidence anchor under a reorg-target prefix; this governs Wave 6 landings, tooling lanes, and every leaf lane. The producer that makes the prohibition mechanical is **installed and fixture-proven in bootstrap T3b**, before any product execution dispatches, and runs blocking in the required admission context continuously from T3b to seal. The **T2→T3b interval is policy-only by construction**: it carries no gate runs and none are claimed. It is closed instead by a retrospective audit executed with the same gate binary in audit mode over the exact commit range and ref-state delta between the T2 authorization record and gate go-live, recorded as durable evidence; any hit must be reverted or dispositioned under Rule 2 before T4, and an audit that is missing, incomplete, or shows unremediated debt fails T4 closed. Behavior is continuous across the boundary even though the enforcement mechanism changes from audited-retrospective to admission-blocking.
2. **Hot-set disposition for target-touching work**, fail-closed default (c): (a) **semantic-port** — after Wave 5 ratifies the inventory, re-express the item in destination shape and land it there; (b) **net-debt-reduction** — before a ratified destination exists, a target-touching landing is admissible only if it strictly reduces target-path surface, proven by a before/after target-surface measurement bound to the census snapshot, introducing no new target-path identifier, crate, path dependency, or exit dependency; (c) **park** with restart evidence. A green admission run never legalizes a new target-path dependency.
3. **Single writer per authority file, one writer per leaf lane.** `/specs/masterplan.json` is the single-writer live plan authority; generated projections are re-derived, never hand-edited. Wave-level concurrency is not lane-level concurrency.
4. **Amendment, not silent correction.** Any delta between a ratified row and realized reality requires an auditable amendment clearing both invariant floors.

### Decision Drivers

1. **The corridor rule inverts the ordering.** Unprovenanced numbers are unratifiable, so freeze is a late prerequisite artifact: survey, census, and measurement all land before the freeze packet, and the packet gates every exit-bearing wave after it.
2. **Path mutation is destructive at estate scale.** Landing the ratified hot set and parking the rest before bulk path mutation is the difference between a bounded restack and an unbounded one — and the temporal contract already fail-closes on unbounded restack and full-estate rebuild.
3. **The live hold is structurally self-blocking.** `planning_entry_contract` is `open` with `binding_plan_approval_allowed=false`, `dispatch_allowed=false`; `execution_wave_dispatch` is `blocked` on `preplanning_authority_closure` with `dispatched_waves` empty; `protected_candidate_merged_green_reviewed=false` and `closed_state_evaluator_proof=false`; the APPROVED review on the closure candidate was `coderabbitai[bot]` with `qualified_human_approval_proven=false`. `ci/facade/cross-artifact-agreement/src/lib.rs::evaluate_preplanning_entry_contract` accepts only `state=open`, `dispatch_allowed=false`, `binding_plan_approval_allowed=false`, `nonbinding_planning_discussion_allowed=true` with a non-empty candidate and three non-empty condition arrays. Closure is a code-and-fixture transition, not a data edit — which is exactly why it is bootstrap work on the CI facade rather than a masterplan_v2 wave.
4. **Encoding voids its own ratification.** `sequencing.founder_ratification.invalidation_rule` voids the ratified digest on any change to dependency-edge endpoints, `work_item_order`, or `execution_waves` ids — exactly what encoding six tracks does.

### Viable Options

**Option A — Bootstrap chain, then Wave 1, with observation lanes concurrent from Wave 1 dispatch (SELECTED).** Closure lands on the CI facade first; Wave 1 encodes masterplan_v2 as the first execution wave; census and baseline observation lanes start at Wave 1 dispatch and bind receipts to canonical ids before freeze; W2–W4 fan out; W5 freezes; W6→W7→W8 serialize; W9 and W10 run concurrently; W11 AND-joins.
- Pros: no authority-bearing artifact precedes its canonical id; reconciliation is live before estate-scale artifacts land; the digest is re-ratified once; the observation/binding split removes revision 1's wall-clock stall without conceding the canonical-id contract.
- Cons: the bootstrap chain is a serialization point with only tooling work running; if the closure amendment cannot pass admission the programme stalls before Wave 1.

**Option B — Pre-dispatch observation (VIABLE contingency; contract authored at bootstrap T2, dormant).** Start census enumeration and baseline measurement between execution authorization (T2) and Wave 1 dispatch (T5), binding receipts retroactively.
- Pros: removes the bootstrap stall; both lanes are observation-only and mutate no product source; the enumerator re-runs before freeze anyway. CRIT-4 is correct that the canonical-id contract forbids *trackers originating authority-bearing work items*, not observation artifacts, and `fact-stale-two-mode-bar-must-be-amended` allows live-tree measurement before the three-mode amendment lands.
- Cons: receipts exist before the id space is extended, so the binding path must be pre-defined rather than improvised.
- **Activation rule (event-based, approval-bearing; no time trigger).** Activates only when BOTH: (i) a named blocking event is recorded — the closure amendment is refused at admission, the qualified-human review path is unavailable, or a stop condition fires on the closure candidate; AND (ii) the founder records an explicit activation approval citing that event.
- **Contract precedence (CRIT-4).** The governing contract exists before any event that can activate it. It is authored at **bootstrap T2** as a durable approval-time repository artifact, Accepted-as-dormant and not in force, fixing the observation-receipt schema, the binding procedure, the rule that a pre-id receipt bound before freeze is not drift, and the fail-closed default that an unbound receipt at freeze is inadmissible. Activation flips that already-existing artifact in force; it is never authored under pressure and never after the event (ARCH-7). Wave 1 later **encodes** the same contract into the MPV2 id space with a canonical id, owner, and reopen rule — a projection of the T2 artifact, verified byte-equivalent to it, not its origin. If Option B activates before Wave 1 exists, receipts produced under it are governed by the T2 artifact from the moment they are produced, bind at the first opportunity after their canonical ids exist, and in all cases before freeze; an unbound receipt at freeze remains inadmissible.

**Option C — Reorg-first spine (INVALIDATED).** Rewriting paths before census closure forces an unbounded restack or full-estate rebuild — both fail-closed — so the temporal track could never terminate, and ref mutation would precede classification. Structurally inadmissible.

**Option D — Freeze-late / measure-on-destination (INVALIDATED).** Deferring freeze strips W6–W10 of the ratified envelopes, hot set, budgets, inventory, and catalog they need as entry criteria; reorg cannot start without a ratified inventory and the inventory is a freeze output. Circular.

### Topology

`from` is prerequisite, `to` is dependent. **Concurrency-eligible sets:** {W2, W3, W4} at depth 1, and **{W9, W10} after W8** — there is no `W9 -> W10` edge and no shared-authority driver justifying one (CRIT-5). **Serialization points:** W1, W5, W6, W7, W8, W11. Wave concurrency is not lane concurrency; writer discipline is fixed in the Ownership and Concurrency Matrix.

**Edge semantics (Architect caveat 1).** `W1 -> W3` and `W1 -> W4` gate those waves' authoritative binding, closure, and exit — **not the start of raw observation**. Observation-only lanes may begin at T5 under Principle 6; their receipts assert no status, mint no work item, and are inadmissible to the corridor until bound to canonical ids, so neither wave can exit or feed freeze on unbound receipts. Every "Wave 1 dispatched" entry criterion in this plan reads the same way: it permits observation to start, never closure to occur.

## Pre-Mortem

### Scenario 1 — Census and hot-set blowup restacks the estate and collapses admission

- **Trigger.** Wave 6 lands before the estate is quiesced, or Wave 7 path mutation begins while a large fraction of the estate is still live. A shared producer or baseline failure is not fixed first, so every leaf restacks against a broken base, each restack triggers a full rebuild rather than an affected-target build, and the admission queue amplifies superlinearly.
- **Blast radius.** Total loss of merge throughput. The temporal track cannot terminate because unbounded restack and full-estate rebuild are both fail-closed. Every downstream wave stalls behind W6. Preservation is endangered because pressure to "just clean up" peaks exactly when classification evidence is weakest.
- **Leading indicator + action.** Restacks per landed item exceeding the ratified bound; push count approaching budget with hot-set members unlanded; conflict rate accelerating; hottest-file contention concentrating; parked-rail admissions falling toward zero while landing attempts rise. **Action:** the temporal owner halts the wave — not escalates it — and the hot set changes only by auditable amendment.
- **Gate.** W6 exit makes fail-closed behavior structural: landing only for ratified members, a green run on a non-member never authorizes a landing, overflow goes to the parked rail rather than a convenience budget amendment, and breaching the restack bound or triggering a full-estate rebuild halts the wave. W7 entry requires W6 quiescence, so path mutation structurally cannot begin against a live estate. Shared producer and baseline failures are fixed before leaf restacks as a W6 ordering rule.

### Scenario 2 — Reorg stalls, dual homes persist, and target debt accretes under the stall

- **Trigger.** A shard opens against rows whose blockers were never cleared — the kernel case is proven mechanical, not speculative: the destination is occupied by a parallel workspace needing opposite single-valued toolchain, target, and edition settings, the codemod does not rewrite `[workspace.dependencies]` path deps, and include-literals dangle under the face layout. The wave partially completes; productive lanes keep delivering; new code lands under target paths "temporarily."
- **Blast radius.** The reorg close test can never pass. The delivery exit is blocked because it must be identified by post-reorg destination identity. The product exit is blocked because Product-100 attaches to `app/<product>/` identities that do not exist. Three of six tracks strand and debt grows monotonically for the duration of the stall.
- **Leading indicator + action.** Dual-home count not decreasing wave over wave; new files under target paths at any time after T2 — refused mechanically from T3b and caught retrospectively by the T2→T3b interval audit; debrand hit count flat or rising; carve-out amendment requests rising as a proxy for fixing the scanner instead of the identifiers. **Action:** the owning shard stops; the blocker elevates to an auditable disposition amendment at Wave 5 authority level; if it cannot clear, the disposition changes — for example two registered roots instead of one — rather than the root closing partially.
- **Gate.** W2 exit requires an admissible single home or a blocking condition with a named unblock item and owner. W5 ratifies every root row as `cleared` or `mutation-frozen` with an owner and unblock item. W7 shards open only on the per-root admission predicate, `mutation-frozen` roots cannot mutate, and global W7 exit stays blocked until zero dual homes remain. Global Binding Rule 1 has bound normatively since T2, its gate has been blocking since T3b, and the T2→T3b interval is audited, so "temporarily" is not available at any point. Carve-out entries require origin proof and are ratified at freeze, so catalog padding is not an escape.

### Scenario 3 — Cutover authority vacuum or CAS cold-start regression at the delivery exit

- **Trigger.** Wave 10 transfers merge authority on a shortened parallel run, or the incumbent admission context is retired before the owned successor is proven. Concurrently the owned CAS shows a cold-cache latency regression on first real workload and the operational reflex is to pre-warm from a known-good machine.
- **Blast radius.** An authority vacuum means nothing can land — and anything that does lands unadmitted, contaminating the evidence chain for every exit depending on merged-commit evidence. A CAS-coupled merge authority fails the independence requirement, so the delivery exit cannot close even after throughput recovers. If the regression manifests only in customer-operated mode, aggregate reporting hides it until the Wave 11 lane rejects the packet — the most expensive discovery point.
- **Leading indicator + action.** Parallel-run duration tracking below the ratified window while cutover preparation proceeds; rollback rehearsal deferred or executed only as documentation; cold-cache tail latency diverging beyond envelope; any operational step requiring a specific machine online for merges; per-mode reporting collapsing into an aggregate; **hosted-CAS bridge usage flat or rising as its ratified expiration approaches**. **Action:** the delivery owner halts further per-mode transfer and the authority map is the binding record of the current state.
- **Gate.** W10 exit requires sustained parallel-run proof before transfer, an exercised rollback rehearsal per mode, explicit proof from the authority map that no interval lacked binding merge authority, proven cold-cache fallback, and structural CAS-independence. Per-mode evidence is required separately for all three modes, so aggregation cannot mask a single-mode failure. The owned successor admission context is established in W7 and the incumbent retires only afterward, making admission-context succession and merge-authority cutover two separately gated transitions rather than one cliff.

## Risks & Mitigations

1. **Estate figures age.** The recorded worktree, branch, detached-head, locked-worktree, and PR counts came from an audit against an hour-old `origin/dev` and will have aged by execution. *Mitigation:* no wave criterion depends on them; they are scale context only. The W3 enumerator produces the authoritative denominator, re-runs on cadence and immediately before freeze, and freeze pins that combined hash.
2. **Blocked kernel move is three work streams, not one data point.** The blockers are proven mechanical: destination occupied by a parallel workspace with irreconcilable edition/toolchain/target pins; codemod not rewriting `[workspace.dependencies]` path deps; include-literals dangling under the face layout. *Mitigation:* the topology decision is a W2 disposition fork ratified at W5; the codemod capability closure is a W1-minted class running concurrent with W2–W6 and fixture-proven before any W7 shard opens; the literal rewrite executes inside W7. Without this decomposition W7's entry criteria are satisfiable while its first move fail-closes.
3. **Sequencing digest invalidation.** Encoding voids the recorded ratification by `invalidation_rule`. *Mitigation:* bootstrap T6 carries explicit re-derivation and fresh ratification; no post-W1 wave dispatches on a stale digest; the stale inline stop-condition literal is normalized onto the ratification record in T3.
4. **Closure depends on an unbuilt evaluator and an open review-admission gap.** `closure_rule` requires a protected evidence-bearing amendment extending the evaluator; the current evaluator deliberately accepts only the open hold; the closure candidate's APPROVED review was a bot. *Mitigation:* bootstrap T3a owns both the evaluator extension and the qualified-human review resolution. If T3a is refused at admission, Option B activates under its event-based, approval-bearing rule against the contract **already authored at T2** — never on a wall-clock timer, and never drafted after the event it governs.
5. **Foundry re-adoption collides with an in-flight purge and live secondary truth.** Doctrine says Intelligence replaces Foundry, program-coverage marks the row `retired-absorbed`, a purge is in flight, and `registry/microservices.json` still carries an `operational-partial` foundry row while two stores enforce a retired-term deny-list. *Mitigation:* the W1 amendment is reciprocally superseding, sequenced after the purge lands, and its scope enumerates every secondary surface plus the deny-list SSOT, so vocab gates neither block the amendment nor leave two live taxonomies unwatched.
6. **No willing structurally independent external operator.** The customer-operated mode is exit-bearing for both substrate and delivery; independence and minimum cell count freeze at W5; a missing census blocks both exits. *Mitigation (synthesized per disposition `conflict:risk-6:ARCH-8:CRIT-6`):* operator recruitment is a first-class work-item class minted at W1 and run from W1 dispatch through W8, with the leading indicator **named prospective operators ≥ ratified minimum by Wave 5** and escalation to the founder below it. If no qualifying operator exists at freeze, the admissible responses are (a) ratify a reachable minimum supported by named prospective operators — prospective status never substitutes for ratified live-cell evidence at exit — or (b) leave freeze open and the affected exits blocked while recruitment and evidence work continue. **Narrowing the mode set is not an admissible response**: all three modes are exit-bearing, W8 forbids narrowing to rescue a stall, and any change would be a new requirements decision outside this plan's approval boundary. An Oyatie-operated substitute is contractually forbidden. Recruitment pressure replaces scope narrowing.
7. **Corridor benchmarks depend on drifting external sources.** Public parity classes change without notice and the citation is half of every ratification's provenance. *Mitigation:* pin each citation to a retrieved snapshot with date and content hash at W4; later upstream changes are post-freeze operational context and cannot move the terminal receipt.
8. **Measurement-governance ambiguity across comparisons.** Cross-run comparisons are meaningful only when commit, configuration, window, suite boundary, numerator, and denominator are pinned. *Mitigation:* W4 exit requires a named boundary per slot; the W10 comparator is per-mode/region/architecture with aggregate rollups explicitly non-authoritative.
9. **Measured-tree versus destination-tree evaluation gap.** Baselines are taken on live trees at W4 and evaluated on destination trees at W8/W10, where path, layout, and build-graph changes shift latency and cost. *Mitigation:* the W5 margin-policy row states that corridor margins account for the measured-tree → destination-tree transfer delta, and the transfer-delta accounting is itself provenanced rather than invented.
10. **Parked-work rot across a long Wave 7.** Restart evidence mitigates recoverability, not rebase cost, and parked items restarted post-reorg pay a path-translation tax. *Mitigation:* the ratified parked-rail capacity row acknowledges that tax explicitly, so capacity is ratified against realistic restart cost rather than nominal count.
11. **Autonomy versus approval gate.** The programme expects autonomous multi-month execution after approval, creating pressure to treat this ralplan as authorization. *Mitigation:* this plan authorizes nothing. T1 approval, T2 execution authorization, and T4 dispatch unblock are three distinct recorded transitions; no lane may self-approve, fabricate independent review, destroy unclassified state, or weaken a gate to obtain green.
12. **Independent verification capacity.** Six exits each need a lane with no implementation involvement, competing for the same capacity that implements. *Mitigation:* reviewer-lane assignment is fixed at W5 alongside the other ratified rows, so independence is designed in rather than reconstructed at W11 when it is most expensive to fix.
