## 2. WHERE I AGREE WITH THE ARCHITECT (adopted, with severity I endorse)

- **FLAG-1 (census 731/105 vs 831/43) — I RAISE to HIGH, not MEDIUM.** The architect rated this MEDIUM; I disagree on severity. This is the single most consequential finding in either review, and here is the principle-grounded reason: the founder rule (plan line 197, decision-record verify-at-each-step) *requires* the verifier to re-read primary sources every iteration and *forbids* trusting a prior verdict. That rule, applied to a self-contradictory primary source, does not degrade gracefully — it **guarantees** one of two failures on the most irreversible lane (831-file mass rename): a permanent verifier stall (reads 105, asserts 43, never reconciles) or a silent ~62-file miscount (reconciles to 105, lets governance-sense files through as intelligence-sense). A mis-routed `oya-foundry-*→oya-intelligence-*` on a governance file is a *newly authored CC-1-class contradiction* — the amendment producing the exact defect it exists to kill. That is not MEDIUM; an irreversible lane with a built-in guaranteed-miscount path is HIGH. **The plan must not enter Wave-0 with this open.**

- **FLAG-2 / Tension A (D-LANES override unstated) — AGREE, MEDIUM.** Confirmed: D-LANES is door:one-way (decision-record:84). The plan overrides its *shape* (serial waves) for sound D-1/D-2 reasons but never names that it is narrowing a one-way founder ruling. Since the plan itself demands founder sign-off for everything touching a one-way door, *narrowing the shape of a one-way organizing principle* must be a named, signed tradeoff in §A/§F — not an implicit resolution. The architect is right; S1/S2 recover most of the lost parallelism, which makes the override smaller and more defensible, but the *naming* is still owed regardless of S1.

- **FLAG-3 / §6 (aspirational asserted-as-real at the canon layer) — AGREE, MEDIUM, with the safety-gate sub-point escalated.** The plan hedges correctly at execution time, but the hedge lives only in a `pending-approval` build plan that gets archived. If archived, "generator is worktree-only" evaporates and the canon (decision-record D1) still asserts a live capability. The architect's fix (S5: promote the aspirational status into the masterplan-wiring meta-ADR + supersede D1 to record generator=worktree-only@2026-06-06) is correct and ruling-compatible (supersede-never-edit). **I escalate the safety-gate instance:** asserting a *live safety enforcement* (D-SAFETY "hooked into D16's runtime Cedar gate") that in reality runs only in a worktree is the highest-consequence aspirational-vs-enforced gap in the plan, because it is a liability/safety commitment (decision-record D-SAFETY door:one-way "safety/liability commitment"). `runtime-hook-pending` labeling is not optional polish here; it is a correctness requirement on a safety claim.

- **§4 carve (consolidation-set must precede per-file rename) — AGREE, this is the sharpest order-defect.** Confirmed against D13 (decision-record:43): the clean ADR-0000+ re-founding "consolidates-provenance, archive old frozen". For any ADR that is both L2-renamed and L1.1-re-founded, the L2 rename on the to-be-archived original is wasted. The plan references the consolidation map inside L1.0 (line 57) but runs L2.2 in Wave-0 *before* L1.1 proves it — so L2 cannot know which files are throwaway. The architect's S2 (freeze the consolidation set as its own door:one-way primary source at the very start of Wave-0, edge `consolidation-set-freeze → {L2.2, L3.3, L1-amend}`) is the correct fix and is the one real ordering correction in either review.

- **§5 gap (cutover_trigger ownerless) — AGREE, MEDIUM.** Confirmed ADR-0510/0250 exist as named triggers (decision-record D-META:26). S4 (`cutover_trigger:` required front-matter pointing at ADR-0510/0250) converts an accreting ownerless "pending" set into a queryable set with named gates. Without it, build-first-cutover-later degrades to build-first-cutover-never and the corpus carries permanent dual-canon. This is a real durability defect over the multi-quarter horizon.

- **§7 hole 3 (no verifier-of-the-MAP) — AGREE, and I rate it HIGH-adjacent.** The L1.0 MAP is door:one-way founder-signed (governance gate), but no *correctness* gate checks it is internally consistent (renumber bijection, consolidation surjection, no id-reuse) before L3.3 and L1.3 build on it. A bad MAP poisons two downstream lanes silently, and the founder sign-off is a governance act, not a bijection check — the founder cannot be expected to hand-verify a 372-row id-bijection. S6 (MAP self-consistency verifier gate ahead of sign-off) is necessary, not nice-to-have.

## 3. WHERE I DIVERGE FROM / EXTEND THE ARCHITECT

- **DIVERGE-1 — The architect under-weights its own FLAG-1 by calling §A "a long proof of something nobody contested."** That framing is rhetorically strong but slightly unfair and, more importantly, it lets the *plan* off the hook for the wrong reason. §A is not wasted: Option-2/Option-3 being ruling-violations does not make documenting *why* they violate rulings worthless — it is the auditable record that the ordering was derived, not assumed (verify-at-each-step applied to the plan's own logic). The real defect is not "§A over-proves order"; it is "§A proves order and **never proves the base data the order operates on is consistent**." I would not tell the founder "the order section is bloat." I would tell the founder "the order section is correct and complete; an equally rigorous *base-data consistency* section (the census reconciliation) is missing and must be added as §A.0." This is a more accurate diagnosis and a cleaner fix than implying §A is padding.

- **DIVERGE-2 — Tension C / FLAG-4 (verifier-as-cohesion-judge self-approval) is real but the architect's fix is incomplete.** The architect correctly spots that the verifier both *produces* the manual cohesion verdict and *is* the only check on it (self-approval, forbidden by decision-record's separate-lane rule). The proposed fix (route the cohesion verdict to a second opus verifier or founder door:one-way) is right but under-specified: a *second verifier reading the same sibling read-set* can replicate the first verifier's blind spot (both miss the same latent contradiction) — two lanes, one failure mode. The stronger fix is to make the manual cohesion stand-in **adversarial**: the second lane's job is not "re-confirm no contradiction" but "find one contradicting atom" (a falsification mandate, not a confirmation mandate). Absent the real cohesion gate, an adversarial second lane is the only thing that approximates the gate's by-construction guarantee. This should be written into the §D L5 gate and §H.1 Failure-3 mitigation, not left as "route to a second verifier."

- **EXTEND-1 (architect missed) — The plan's exit criteria (§G) are not all TESTABLE; one is unfalsifiable as written.** §G exit line: "zero surviving canon-contradictions" and "the cohesion read-set shows zero contradicting atoms." On a corpus where the cohesion gate is [ASPIRATIONAL] (not on dev) and the stand-in is a manual verifier read, "zero contradicting atoms" is asserted by the same lane that would have to find them — there is no independent oracle. An exit criterion you cannot independently falsify is not a testable acceptance criterion; it is an assertion. **Required:** §G must define the *evidence artifact* that proves "zero contradictions" — e.g., a published cohesion read-set diff + the adversarial second-lane's falsification attempt log showing zero atoms found after a real search — not merely a verifier's green. This is the plan's own verify-at-each-step rule turned on its own exit gate.

- **EXTEND-2 (architect missed) — The DROP decisions lack a verification step.** The plan drops ADRs 0325/0316/0349-Jenkins-half (line 66, decision-record:46) and DROPs Bominal Train (line 102, decision-record:98). The §D/§H.2 evidence tables verify what was *authored/renamed/restored*, but there is **no gate that verifies a DROP did not leave dangling inbound references.** Dropping 0316 (superseded by 0329) is safe only if nothing still `supersedes:`/`amends:`/cites 0316 as live. The integrity sweep (L3.3) verifies dangling *outbound* edges against the frozen MAP, but a DROP creates *inbound* orphans that L3.3 (run in Wave-0, before the L1.2 Proposed-batch DROPs in Wave-1) does not re-check. **Required:** add an L1.2 DROP-verification gate — for each dropped id, a corpus-wide inbound-reference resolver must show zero live citations (or each citation re-pointed to the superseding id) *after* the drop. This is a real hole: the founder ruling D14 (decision-record:46) authorizes the drops but the plan never proves the drops are reference-safe.

- **EXTEND-3 (architect missed) — CC-9 eventing has a latent contradiction the plan may re-introduce.** Plan line 85 says "CC-9 eventing 3-way (Pulsar canon; scope-or-drop NATS/Redpanda) — **reconcile against ADR-0005**." But decision-record D-EVENT (line ~107 region) rules Pulsar canonical and says the NATS-JetStream+Redpanda framing is STALE → amend to Pulsar. The plan's "reconcile against ADR-0005" introduces a dependency the plan does not resolve: if ADR-0005 itself names a different substrate, the reconciliation direction is unspecified (does ADR-0005 win, or does D-EVENT?). D-EVENT is the founder ruling and is door:one-way, so D-EVENT must win — but the plan writing "reconcile against ADR-0005" without stating the precedence invites the verifier to treat ADR-0005 as co-equal. **Required:** state explicitly that D-EVENT (Pulsar) is the precedence authority and ADR-0005 is amended to it, not "reconciled with" it.

## 6. BOTTOM LINE

The plan is **order-correct and well-defended** — the wave ordering satisfies all three RULED ordering constraints, the L2-before-L1 edge is genuinely right (rename-before-reauthor avoids double-work on immutable docs), and §H is a real deliberate-mode pre-mortem + verification matrix. The architect review is evidence-grounded with no phantom findings, and I independently re-verified its four headline claims as TRUE against primary sources.

I downgrade APPROVE-WITH-CONDITIONS to **ITERATE** for one principled reason: two of the conditions (G1 census reconciliation, G2 consolidation-set freeze) are **not optional polish — they are hard predicates the irreversible lanes run against**, and they are absent from the plan as written. A plan that enters Wave-0 with a self-contradictory census on its 831-file mass-rename lane will, by its own verify-at-each-step rule, either stall or silently author the exact CC-1 contradiction the amendment exists to kill. That is a data-correctness gate, not a wording nit. Plus three holes the architect missed (EXTEND-1 unfalsifiable exit, EXTEND-2 missing DROP gate, EXTEND-3 CC-9 precedence) touch the mandated testable-criteria bar.

None of G1–G5 block founder sign-off on the *sequencing logic* (§A) — that logic is sound. They are pre-Wave-0 conditions on the *base data, the meta-decision naming, and the missing correctness gates* the lanes will run against. Apply G1–G5 (especially G1 and G2 before Wave-0) and fold R1–R6, and the next round is APPROVE.

VERDICT: ITERATE
