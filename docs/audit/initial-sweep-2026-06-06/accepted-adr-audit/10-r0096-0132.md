**Consequence:** the supervisor cluster's *crate-name home* (`oya-foundry-*`) is dead per the 831-ref foundry rename (canon:99); its *agentic-VCS pipeline dependency* (ADR-0110/0111/0112/0113) is retired/superseded; and the "Siigari/claude-heartbeat multi-account CLI supervisor" product itself overlaps the declined agent-execution-controller.
