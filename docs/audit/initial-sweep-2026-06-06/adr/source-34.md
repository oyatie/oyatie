### ADR-0295 — Bootstrap CI SPIFFE Identity + T+8h Kill-Switch
- **decision_atom:** Every Stage-1 external CI runner that bootstraps the platform's trust chain receives a SPIFFE SVID from a one-shot offline-rooted bootstrap CA (key destroyed at end of Stage-1), every Stage-1 artifact carries a sigstore-cosign/Rekor SLSA-L3 attestation, and a hard T+8h Cedar kill-switch fragment (org-root-signed) forbids all Stage-1-rooted trust regardless of Stage-2 readiness — closing the highest-value bootstrap-compromise window.
- **domain:** security-supplychain (cross-cuts: crypto-keymgmt)
- **current_status:** Proposed
- **disposition:** AMEND (then RATIFY) — sound and high-quality; needs ref/scoping touch-ups (see truth_flag + tensions). Mechanically this is the precondition gate for ADR-0247's own promotion.
- **proposed_resolution:** RATIFY — this is the named resolution for CRITICAL finding F5-247-02; the threat model (compromised-CI-runner) is real and the SPIFFE + one-shot-CA + cosign + kill-switch composition is industry-canonical. Do not DROP. Ratify after the AMEND fixes land.
- **governing:** n/a (no supersession)
- **truth_flag:** PARTIAL — the *security design* is TRUE/aligned, but the document is laden with retired-vocab and unbuilt-substrate assumptions: principals are named `oyatie.foundry.bootstrap-*` (foundry RETIRED per ADR-0335/0347 → should be `oyatie.intelligence.*`); schema is raw Postgres DDL (fine under SOURCE best-of-breed, but conflicts with LINUX ADR-0001's own-DB posture on merge); depends on OpenBao/Cedar/audit-chain substrates that are themselves still Proposed. The bootstrap CA `$40K–60K/ceremony` + 11-participant in-person ceremony is a genuine cost the masterplan must carry, not slop.
- **in_masterplan:** PARTIAL — carries `requires_amendment_to: ADR-0247/0243`, `closes_findings`, `enforced_by` gate list, and `keystone_position`. Strong planning_impact signal but it binds *into* other ADRs rather than declaring its own masterplan_ref; under the **generated-from-ADRs** reading it feeds the plan via supersede/amend edges; under **masterplan-is-authority** it needs an explicit `masterplan_ref` (currently absent).
- **tensions:** (1) `oyatie.foundry.bootstrap-*` namespace vs ADR-0335/0347 foundry→intelligence retirement — naming drift. (2) Couples to ADR-0247 (Proposed) + ADR-0293/0294 (meta-trust-root, fragment-soak) which are themselves un-ratified — a Proposed-on-Proposed dependency chain. (3) ASC-04 sovereign-mode bootstrap explicitly left OPEN. (4) HSM may be *shared* with ADR-0293 meta-trust-root — slot-partition assumption is load-bearing and untested.
- **hyperscaler_challenge:** ALIGNED — Google (BeyondProd/Borg SLSA, Binary Authorization), AWS (Signer + Nitro), and the CNCF/sigstore ecosystem all do exactly SPIFFE-identity + transparency-log-attested supply chain.
