## RALPLAN-DR

### Principles (5)
1. **Reuse the detection LOGIC, never re-derive policy — but the reuse vector differs per discipline.** Three shapes, in priority order: (a) a **pure lib** where one exists — `oya-check-layered-architecture-discipline` (`libs/oya-check-layered-architecture-discipline/src/lib.rs`), `oya-check-data-class` (`libs/oya-check-data-class/src/lib.rs`), and **`oya-governance-predictable-naming-kernel::check()`** (the FULL pure layer-suffix/role predicate — `oya-` prefix + no-uppercase + declared-role ∈ `ALLOWED_ROLES` (closed 13-value enum, `:32`) + role/suffix match + `oya-check-*` carve-out — `libs/oya-governance-predictable-naming-kernel/src/lib.rs:164`, FS-free, currently UNWIRED) — imported as a dependency; (b) a **pure domain crate** where one exists — cargo-prefix → `oya-intelligence-cargo-prefix-domain::validate_cargo_prefix` (already imported at `oya-dev-cli/src/lib.rs:31`: `use oya_intelligence_cargo_prefix_domain::{CargoPrefixMember, validate_cargo_prefix};`); (c) ELSE **extract a pure predicate OUT of the impure dev-cli arm and re-home the FS/git I/O into the producer `collect_*`** — this is the case for manifest-hygiene (no pure crate owns the full §2.5#7 field-set), and for the §2.5#5 hexagonal IMPORT-MATRIX (`oya-dev-cli/src/commands/gate/architecture_boundaries.rs::allowed_dependency_roles()`, impure: hardcoded crate lists + FS walks). **DO NOT conflate §2.5#4 (BNF layer-suffix, S1) with §2.5#5 (import-matrix):** S1's logic is the pure `check()` above (shape a, unit-diffable); `architecture_boundaries.rs` is the SEPARATE import-matrix gate (shape c, backfill/advisory). In all three shapes the policy is REUSED, not re-derived; only the I/O boundary moves. All three are surfaced through the thin `evaluate_keyed -> BTreeSet<Finding{code,key}>` adapter. The equivalence-proof rung (below) is what MAKES "reuse" auditable for shape (c) where no pure `pub fn` exists to unit-diff.
2. **Born-blocking but self-DoS-proof.** A newly-wired gate must baseline TODAY's violations (shrink-only ratchet) and be proven GREEN locally on the live tree BEFORE it is wired into the blocking `oya-ci-required` context. Never push a RED gate to the live firewall.
3. **One canonical blocking context.** The migration builds to `oya-ci-required` (single required status on `dev`), never to per-gate contexts. The constituent gate lanes fan in; branch protection keys only the one name (the HTTP-501 multi-producer-deadlock lesson is binding — verified in `.github/workflows/oya-ci-required.yml:9-11`).
4. **A MINIMAL conformance floor BEFORE import; the rest backfills.** The pre-M-lane floor is bounded to the minimal subset {S1 BNF layer-suffix §2.5#4, S2 manifest-hygiene §2.5#7} — the two import-time structural policies, each a single serial producer fold (see §3a producer-contention). These must be LIVE before the M-lanes import ~225 sibling crates, else non-conformant code lands and sprawl recurs (Task #7 strict-ordering precondition, MIGRATION-PLAN-RESYNC §3 / `MIGRATION-PLAN-RESYNC.md:136` STEP-7). The remaining blocking gates (cargo-prefix, retired-vocab, data_class, slot2) are post-import backfill — they guard per-PR drift the firewall catches regardless of import order, and slot2 is `needs-infra` (cannot baseline until services exist).
5. **Keep-posture with justification.** Keep a check only with a clear reason mapped to §2.5 or ADR-0056. Duplicates of already-live firewall gates are DROPped, not re-ported.

### Decision Drivers (top 3)
1. **D1 — Sprawl-recurrence risk is the dominant cost.** ~225 crates land via the M-lanes; without a live conformance floor they land non-conformant. The plan's value is front-loaded onto the cheapest-highest-impact gates (BNF layer-suffix, manifest hygiene) being LIVE first.
2. **D2 — Self-DoS of the live `oya-ci-required` is the dominant hazard.** The firewall is already the single required status on `dev` (HEAD `613796d61`). A too-strict port REDs the existing tree and blocks ALL merges. Baseline-existing + local-green-before-wire is non-negotiable.
3. **D3 — Dedupe against 5 already-live gates.** `cloud-ci-brand-residue`, `cloud-ci-retired`-residue logic, supply-chain overlap already exist in the firewall (verified in committed `gate-baseline.generated.json`). Re-porting them would create the duplicate-logic hazard (pre-mortem B).

### Viable Options (≥2) — OVERALL APPROACH

**Option A — "Port-all-canonical-then-migrate" (port every KEEP gate before the first M-lane).**
- Pros: Maximum conformance coverage at import time; no backfill debt.
- Cons: Large blast radius; the biggest reshape (hexagonal import-matrix, §2.5#5) blocks everything; high self-DoS surface (every gate must be baselined at once); long critical path before any M-lane can start.
- Verdict: REJECTED as the default — couples cheap gates to the expensive hexagonal reshape.

**Option B — "Minimal-blocking-set-then-migrate-then-backfill" (CHOSEN).**
- Tier-1 BLOCKING set wired first (the cheap, high-value, already-backed gates: BNF layer-suffix, manifest-hygiene, cargo-prefix, retired-vocab beyond word-scan, data-class). Then M-lanes start. ADVISORY + heavier gates (hexagonal import-matrix, slot2 registration) backfill in parallel / promote later via the ratchet.
- Pros: Conformance floor LIVE fastest; smallest self-DoS surface per increment; the expensive hexagonal reshape does not block the cheap floor; each port is an independent, RED/GREEN-proven increment.
- Cons: A window where some §2.5 requirements are advisory-only (mitigated: M-lanes are founder-gated and sequenced; backfill lands before L1 office).
- Verdict: CHOSEN. Matches the firewall's existing shrink-only-ratchet model and the surface-all fan-in.

**Option C (orthogonal) — "One mega conformance-gate crate" vs "one crate per check."**
- Mega-crate: fewer BUCK targets, one `needs:` edge; but one RED fixture fails ALL gates (destroys per-lane attribution), harder dedupe, and a mega-crate DEFEATS the directory-driven `gate_registration.rs` auto-discovery. (The "couples unrelated logic" objection is intentionally NOT the load-bearing argument here — see ADR Alternatives: the producer is already a coupling monolith, so the real benefits of one-crate-per-gate are auto-policing + independent RED attribution, not producer isolation.)
- One-crate-per-check (CHOSEN sub-decision): mirrors the 5 live gates exactly (`oya-cloud-ci-total-accounting-app` etc.); each gate is independently baselined, RED/GREEN-fixtured, auto-discovered by `gate_registration.rs`, and fanned-in with attributable RED; isolates self-DoS to one lane.
- Verdict: one-crate-per-canonical-gate, named `oya-cloud-ci-<discipline>-app`, reusing the existing detection LOGIC per Principle 1 (pure lib/domain dep where one exists; else extract-pure-predicate + re-home I/O into the producer).

### Pre-mortem (4 scenarios) + mitigations
- **(a) Ported check too strict → REDs the existing tree → self-DoS of the LIVE `oya-ci-required`.** The firewall is the single required status on `dev`; a RED port blocks every merge org-wide. MITIGATION: (1) baseline-existing — fold TODAY's violations into `build_gate_baseline` with `mode: baseline-block-on-new` so only NEW keys block (proven pattern: total-accounting `unaccounted` keys + `registry_drift` frozen_empty); (2) local-green-before-wire — run `cargo test -p oya-cloud-ci-<gate>-app` AND the born-blocking self-test on the live corpus, prove GREEN, BEFORE adding the `needs:` edge; (3) never-push-RED — the workflow edge is the LAST step, gated on a committed==regenerated byte-parity check.
- **(b) Duplicate logic between a ported check and an existing firewall gate.** `brand-residue` is BOTH a live firewall gate (`cloud-ci-brand-residue`, verified in baseline) AND an `oya-check-brand-residue` lib AND a dev-cli `gate validate brand-residue` arm. Porting it again = two sources of truth diverging. MITIGATION: the INVENTORY step (Step 1) produces an explicit OVERLAP column; any check whose discipline already has a live `cloud-ci-*` gate-id is DROP (re-port) — the live gate already consumes the shared lib (the producer imports `oya_check_brand_residue::forbidden_vocab`, verified `main.rs:26`). The §2.5#11 "rebrand-arrow/retired-terms scan" is satisfied by promoting the EXISTING live brand-residue + the `oya-check-retired-vocabulary` lib into one gate, not a new scanner.
- **(c) A DROPped check was actually load-bearing.** Dropping a check that some downstream consumer (a CI job, another gate's input) silently depends on breaks that consumer. MITIGATION: before any DROP, grep the source for the check's command string / lib symbol across `.github/workflows/`, `scripts/`, and other `oya-check-*` / gate crates; a DROP requires zero live consumers OR an explicit replacement edge. (Concrete instance found: `backbone-microservices-ci.yml:305` calls `./bin/oya gate validate cargo-prefix` — cargo-prefix is therefore load-bearing and its retirement must be sequenced AFTER its firewall replacement is live; see Step 4.)
- **(d) Producer serialization stalls the conformance floor / a fold conflict corrupts the baseline.** Every baseline fold mutates the SHARED producer (`GateInputs` fixed struct `main.rs:114`, `GATE_IDS:[&str;5]` `lib.rs:462`, the single `build_gate_baseline` call `main.rs:121`) behind one founder signoff door — so concurrent S-gate folds merge-conflict, and a botched array-length / struct-field edit silently drops a gate from the regenerated baseline. The failure mode: the M-lane import precondition slips because S1-S6 cannot land in parallel, OR a conflict-resolution error regenerates a baseline missing a gate-id and nobody notices until a violation lands unguarded. MITIGATION: (1) reduce the critical-path floor to the MINIMAL {S1, S2} subset (§3a) so only 2 serial folds + 2 signoff doors gate the M-lanes; (2) serialize the producer folds explicitly — one fold per commit, one signoff per commit, never two in flight against `GateInputs`/`GATE_IDS`; (3) the `gate_registration.rs` directory-driven tests (`tests/gate_registration.rs:68,:122`) auto-discover the gate crate and fail RED if a fold landed a crate but the baseline/workflow lost its registration — catching the "silently dropped gate-id" corruption; (4) the committed==regenerated byte-parity check (registry-drift / cross-artifact) catches a baseline that no longer matches a clean regen.

### Expanded Test Plan (deliberate mode)
- **Unit** (per ported gate crate): RED fixture (≥1 synthetic violation → ≥1 `Finding`), GREEN fixture (clean input → empty `BTreeSet<Finding>`), `evaluate()` == bare-code projection of `evaluate_keyed()` (the total-accounting invariant, `total-accounting/src/lib.rs:99-108`). Keys are stable + deterministic (sorted, git-ls-files-rooted).
- **Behavioral-equivalence oracle (MANDATORY, per ported discipline) — this is the proof that "reuse" is real.** For each port, the NEW gate's key-set, run against TODAY's live corpus, MUST equal the OLD CLI's finding-set on the same corpus, recorded verbatim as the equivalence proof in the Step-1/Step-2 artifact. Two regimes by Principle-1 shape:
  - **Shapes (a)/(b) — a pure `pub fn` exists** (layered-architecture, data-class, **predictable-naming-kernel `check()` = S1 BNF layer-suffix**, cargo-prefix): unit-diff the new gate's `evaluate_keyed` keys against the pure lib/domain fn output on identical input. Byte-set equality.
  - **Shapes (c) — NO pure `pub fn` exists** (manifest-hygiene §2.5#7; and the §2.5#5 hexagonal import-matrix `architecture_boundaries.rs` when it backfills): the diff is **end-to-end** — capture the OLD `oya gate validate <lane>` stdout finding-set on the live tree, normalize to keys, and assert it == the new gate's `evaluate_keyed` key-set. Recorded as the equivalence proof BEFORE the old arm is retired. This is the only auditable substitute for a unit-diff when the source logic is entangled with I/O.
- **Integration** (producer): the new gate's `collect_*` input builder is folded into `build_gate_baseline`; regenerating faces is idempotent (run twice → byte-identical); the committed `gate-baseline.generated.json` `gates:{<new-gate-id>:{...}}` entry == regenerated (byte-parity test in registry-drift / cross-artifact).
- **e2e** (firewall): born-blocking self-test — the gate, run against the LIVE corpus today, produces exactly the baselined key set (GREEN); a deliberately-injected NEW violation key produces RED (ratchet-growth blocked); a deliberately-removed key is allowed (shrink-only). The fan-in `oya-ci-required` is green IFF the new lane is green.
- **Observability**: the gate emits a counts line (`N scanned, M findings, K blocking`) mirroring the existing gates' stdout convention; the baseline carries `_provenance` + `source_inputs_digest` so a drift is attributable. **CRITICAL DISTINCTION (must-assert):** a baselined NEW-key finding MUST surface as a RED fan-in lane in `oya-ci-required` with the attributable offending key visible in the CI output — i.e. the firewall lane goes RED and prints which `Finding{code,key}` is new. This is DISTINCT from the existing dev-cli REPORT-ONLY advisory counts lines (`oya-dev-cli/src/commands/gate/mod.rs:869-975`, e.g. `authz-tier discipline advisory: … {} findings`) which merely PRINT a count and exit SUCCESS — never blocking. The test rung asserts the NEW-key path is blocking-with-attribution, not report-only: inject a new key, confirm the lane RED-s AND the key appears in the failure output.
- **Registration meta-test** (`gate_registration.rs`): the two directory-driven tests in `cloud/cloud-ci/gates/oya-cloud-ci-firewall-app/tests/gate_registration.rs` — `every_gate_crate_is_registered_in_oya_ci_required_workflow` (`:68`) and `every_gate_lane_is_a_dependency_of_the_fan_in_job` (`:122`) — auto-discover the new gate dir and fail RED if it is not registered as a workflow job AND wired as a fan-in `needs:` dependency. This is the safety-net that catches a ported gate left un-wired. (It is NOT the `cloud-ci-automation-ratchet` gate, which evaluates the automation matrix — see Port-Pattern step 6.)

### ADR block

> **ADR (proposed): Migrate the canonical CLI-governance check subset into the Phase-0 firewall ratchet, retire the oya-dev-cli gate surface.**
>
> - **Decision.** Migrate the canonical subset of the 72 `oya-check-*` libs + the `oya-dev-cli gate validate` lanes into one-crate-per-discipline firewall gates (`oya-cloud-ci-<discipline>-app`) using `evaluate_keyed -> BTreeSet<Finding{code,key}>`, REUSING the existing detection logic per Principle 1 (pure lib/domain dep where one exists; else extract-pure-predicate + re-home FS/git I/O into the producer `collect_*`), each port carrying a recorded behavioral-equivalence oracle (unit-diff for pure shapes; end-to-end old-CLI-vs-new-keys for impure shapes). Fold each into the producer's `build_gate_baseline` (baseline TODAY's violations, block only NEW), fan into the single `oya-ci-required` context, and retire the `oya check / oya gate / oya verify` CLI surface once its live consumers are replaced AND the Step-4.0 branch-protection `gh api` precondition passes. Sequence cheapest-highest-value first (Option B), with the MANDATORY pre-M-lane floor bounded to the MINIMAL {S1 BNF layer-suffix, S2 manifest-hygiene} subset (producer serialization, §3a); cargo-prefix/retired-vocab/data_class/slot2 are post-import backfill, and slot2 is a NET-NEW detector (no existing logic) that may land as backfill-blocking or ADVISORY per Step 1.
> - **Drivers.** D1 sprawl-recurrence before the ~225-crate import; D2 self-DoS of the live required context; D3 dedupe against 5 already-live gates.
> - **Alternatives considered.**
>   - *Option A — port-all-canonical-then-migrate.* Rejected: couples the cheap floor gates to the expensive hexagonal reshape (§2.5#5) and maximizes the simultaneous self-DoS surface.
>   - *Option C — one mega conformance-gate crate.* Rejected. The naive "coupling" objection is ASYMMETRIC and must be confronted: the producer (`oya-cloud-ci-accounting-registry-app`) is ALREADY a coupling monolith — one crate holds `GateInputs` (`main.rs:114`), `GATE_IDS` (`lib.rs:462`), and the single `build_gate_baseline` call (C2b: gate INPUTS are already centralized, so "isolate the producer" is not the real win). The genuine reason one-crate-per-gate beats a mega-crate is therefore NOT producer isolation but: (i) the directory-driven `gate_registration.rs` meta-tests (`tests/gate_registration.rs:68,:122`) AUTO-POLICE each gate dir — a mega-crate defeats per-gate auto-discovery; (ii) independent RED attribution — a mega-crate's single test target turns "gate X RED" into "the whole conformance crate RED," destroying per-lane fan-in attribution that the 5 live gates rely on; (iii) it mirrors the proven 5-live-gate structure exactly (`oya-cloud-ci-<gate>-app`). So C is rejected on auto-policing + attribution, not on a coupling argument the producer already violates.
>   - *Alt-D — run the existing §2.5 CLI per-M-lane PR at full strength (instead of front-wiring baselined firewall gates).* Rejected. `MIGRATION-PLAN-RESYNC.md:136` STEP-7 already specifies a CLI-per-lane conformance path, so this is the incumbent alternative, not a strawman. THE M1 PARADOX, confronted head-on: a baselined `mode: baseline-block-on-new` gate enforces strictly LESS at import than a full-strength per-PR CLI run (the CLI flags ALL ~110 BNF violations; the baselined gate freezes them and blocks only NEW keys). So why is the weaker-at-M1 option chosen? Because the comparison is not "strength at M1" but "durable structural enforcement across ALL future PRs": (1) the firewall gate is a DURABLE structural enforcer keyed into the single required `oya-ci-required` context — it gates every PR forever, not just the migration window; the per-lane CLI check DISAPPEARS after migration, leaving zero standing enforcement against drift-back; (2) one canonical required context (Driver D2 / HTTP-501 lesson) vs N per-lane ad-hoc checks that each branch-protection would have to learn; (3) the shrink-only ratchet guarantees monotone debt burndown (ADR C2) whereas a per-PR CLI at full strength would BLOCK the M-lanes on pre-existing debt (re-introducing the Option-A coupling failure). The baselined gate is intentionally weaker at M1 to be infinitely more durable and self-DoS-safe afterward; the equivalence-proof rung (Expanded Test Plan) ensures the baselined gate still DETECTS exactly what the CLI did, it just FREEZES rather than blocks the pre-existing set.
> - **Why chosen.** Option B + one-crate-per-gate minimizes self-DoS surface per increment, gets the conformance floor LIVE fastest, and mirrors the proven 5-gate firewall structure exactly.
> - **Consequences.**
>   - *(C1) Baselined BLOCKING gates enforce "no NEW violations," NOT "§2.5-clean."* This is the explicit, accepted semantic of `mode: baseline-block-on-new`: TODAY's violations are folded into the gate's `keys` array as the frozen baseline; only keys NOT in the baseline block a PR. Therefore the existing ~110 BNF layer-suffix debt (HYPOTHESIS, Step-1 recompute) is FROZEN — shrink-only — at import time; it is NOT paid down before the M-lanes start. Imported crates are gated on NOVELTY (a new crate adding a non-canonical layer suffix RED-s; pre-existing debt stays). This is the only self-DoS-safe posture for a single required context that already gates `dev` (Driver D2).
>   - *(C2) Debt-burndown is a separate, committed lane — not a precondition.* The frozen BNF debt is burned down post-floor via the shrink-only ratchet (every PR that touches a debt crate is nudged to fix it; the baseline can only shrink). COMMITMENT: the BNF baseline key-count must reach zero before L1 office (tied to the same gate as S7-S8 backfill in §5), tracked as a burndown line in the Step-1 artifact. Rationale for accept-and-defer: paying down ~110 violations before import would couple the cheap floor to a large reshape (the Option-A failure mode) and delay the M-lanes; freezing + shrink-only gets the floor LIVE fastest while guaranteeing monotone improvement.
>   - *(C3) Transient advisory window + staged retirement.* A transient window where some §2.5 requirements are advisory-only (acceptable: M-lanes are founder-gated/sequenced). The dev-cli retirement is staged behind replacement-live (no live break). The ratchet baseline grows by ~5-7 gate-ids.
> - **Follow-ups.** (F1) hexagonal import-matrix (§2.5#5) is the biggest reshape — own ADR + own lane, promoted from advisory after the monolith split; (F2) the `dev` branch-protection `gh api` confirmation is PROMOTED from a follow-up to the Step-4.0 HARD GATE (PASS-REQUIRED before any dev-cli retirement edit — see §4); (F3) the catalog `AGGREGATED_VALIDATE_LANES` (~120 lanes) needs a retired-vs-migrated reconciliation pass folded into `registry/vocabulary/retired.yaml`; (F4) slot2 (§2.5#6) is a NET-NEW detector with no existing logic to reuse — its blocking-vs-advisory disposition + RED/GREEN-fixture design is a Step-1 decision; (F5) the BNF-debt burndown-to-zero commitment (Consequence C2) is tracked as a burndown line in the Step-1 artifact, due before L1 office.

## 3. Port Pattern (how ONE canonical check moves)

Grounded in the live exemplars (`oya-cloud-ci-total-accounting-app/src/lib.rs` + `oya-cloud-ci-accounting-registry-app/src/main.rs`):

1. **New crate** `cloud/cloud-ci/gates/oya-cloud-ci-<discipline>-app/`, reusing the existing detection logic per Principle 1 — depend on the pure `oya-check-<discipline>` lib (shape a) OR the pure domain crate `oya-intelligence-cargo-prefix-domain` (shape b) where one exists; ELSE extract a pure predicate OUT of the impure `oya-dev-cli` arm and re-home its FS/git I/O into the producer `collect_*` (shape c). In ALL shapes: do NOT re-derive policy; the equivalence-oracle (step below + Step-2 acceptance) PROVES the reuse.
2. **Adapter**: `pub fn evaluate_keyed(fixture: &Value) -> BTreeSet<Finding>` where `Finding{code: String, key: String}` (the verified contract, `total-accounting/src/lib.rs:58-62`). `key` = the offending unit (crate name / path / decision id), deterministic + sorted. `pub fn evaluate(&Value) -> Report` is the bare-code projection (`total-accounting/src/lib.rs:99-108`) so there is ONE source of truth.
2b. **Behavioral-equivalence oracle (MANDATORY, before fan-in)**: record that the new `evaluate_keyed` key-set on TODAY's live corpus == the OLD detection's finding-set. Pure shapes (a)/(b): unit-diff vs the lib/domain `pub fn`. Impure shape (c): capture the OLD `oya gate validate <lane>` stdout, normalize to keys, assert equality end-to-end. This is the auditable proof of "reuse, not re-derive."
3. **Producer fold**: add a `collect_<discipline>(repo_root, inputs) -> Value` input builder (model after `collect_brand_residue`, `main.rs:247` — the corpus-scan exemplar that imports `oya_check_brand_residue::forbidden_vocab`), pass it into `build_gate_baseline`, emit `gates:{cloud-ci-<discipline>:{<code>:{mode:"baseline-block-on-new", keys:[…TODAY's violations…], frozen_empty:false}}}`.
4. **Baseline-existing**: the keys array is seeded with TODAY's violation keys so only NEW keys grow the ratchet (the proven shrink-only model). For corpus-binary predicates use `frozen_empty:true` + the `<gate>` sentinel key (`total-accounting/src/lib.rs:76`).
5. **Workflow job + fan-in**: add `gate-<discipline>` job to `oya-ci-required.yml` (mirror `gate-total-accounting:` block, lines 81-95) AND add its `needs:` edge to the fan-in job. Branch protection is untouched (single context).
6. **`gate_registration` meta-test (the registration safety-net)**: `cloud/cloud-ci/gates/oya-cloud-ci-firewall-app/tests/gate_registration.rs` carries two directory-driven tests — `every_gate_crate_is_registered_in_oya_ci_required_workflow` (`:68`) and `every_gate_lane_is_a_dependency_of_the_fan_in_job` (`:122`). These AUTO-DISCOVER new gate directories under `cloud/cloud-ci/gates/` and fail RED if a discovered gate crate is not registered as a workflow job AND wired as a `needs:` dependency of the fan-in job. (NOTE: this is the registration net — distinct from the GATE-4 automation-ratchet gate `cloud-ci-automation-ratchet`, which evaluates the automation matrix, NOT crate registration. Do not conflate them.)

### 3a. Producer Contention (the serialization point — bounds the critical path)

The per-gate crate (`oya-cloud-ci-<discipline>-app`) is independent, BUT every baseline fold (step 3 above) edits the SHARED producer crate `oya-cloud-ci-accounting-registry-app`, and three of its sites are single-writer hot-spots:
- **`GateInputs` is a FIXED struct** (DEFINED at `accounting-registry-app/src/lib.rs:476`; instantiated as a literal at `src/main.rs:114`) with one named field per gate (`total_accounting`, `cross_artifact`, `automation_ratchet`, `staleness`, `brand_residue`). Adding a gate means editing BOTH the def and the literal — a textual edit every other in-flight fold also touches.
- **`GATE_IDS: [&str; 5]`** is the canonical id list in the producer LIB (`accounting-registry-app/src/lib.rs:462`), iterated by the baseline builder. Adding a gate-id mutates this array (and its `[&str; N]` length) — another single-line contention point.
- **`build_gate_baseline(&gate_inputs)`** is the single call site (`main.rs:121`) folding all gate inputs into one baseline artifact, gated by the one-way founder signoff door (`gate-baseline.signoff.json`) on each baseline mutation.

**Consequence:** S1..S6 do NOT parallelize cleanly. They serialize through (i) one producer crate (merge-conflict on `GateInputs` + `GATE_IDS` + `build_gate_baseline`) and (ii) one founder signoff door per baseline-growing commit. The per-gate-crate authoring parallelizes; the producer fold + signoff is a serial pipeline.

**Critical-path decision (chosen): reduce the pre-M-lane floor to a NAMED MINIMAL subset rather than cost the full S1-S6 serial path against the Task-#7 precondition.** The mandatory pre-M-lane (pre-Task-#7) BLOCKING floor is the **MINIMAL subset {S1 BNF layer-suffix, S2 manifest-hygiene}** — the two cheapest-highest-value gates that (a) directly police the ~225 incoming crates' structural conformance (§2.5#4 + §2.5#7, the two policies most likely to be violated at import), and (b) have the smallest self-DoS surface to baseline first. **S3 cargo-prefix, S4 retired-vocab-into-brand-residue, S5 data_class, S6 slot2 are POST-IMPORT BACKFILL** — folded into the producer serially after the minimal floor is live, landing before L1 office (the heaviest non-hexagonal sibling). This bounds the serial producer pipeline on the critical path to 2 folds + 2 signoff doors (S1, S2) rather than 6, removing the contention objection from the M-lane gating decision. (Rationale: §2.5#4/#7 are the import-time conformance floor; #6 slot2 is anyway `needs-infra` — it cannot baseline until the M-lanes ADD services, so it was never a true pre-import gate; cargo-prefix/data-class/retired-vocab guard drift that the firewall catches per-PR regardless of import order.)

**Honest concession + Step-1 floor-membership decision (cargo-prefix):** cargo-prefix (S3) is LOAD-BEARING at full strength TODAY (`backbone-microservices-ci.yml:305`). Deferring it to post-import backfill would make the migration window strictly WEAKER on cargo-prefix than the status quo CLI. Because S3 is shape (b) pure (`oya-intelligence-cargo-prefix-domain::validate_cargo_prefix`, lowest authoring cost) + LOW self-DoS, it is a STRONG candidate for promotion into the pre-M-lane floor → **{S1, S2, S3}** (one extra serial fold + door). Step-1 makes the final floor-membership call; if S3 stays backfill, the accepted transient regression must be stated and the backfill must land before L1 office. (This is the only deferred discipline currently enforced at full strength; data_class + retired-vocab were never import-blocking, so deferring those carries no regression.)
