# Ask and Use of Funds

## 01 - Financing Summary

- Oyatie is seeking $18.0 million in seed financing.
- The proposed round is sized for 24 months of execution runway.
- The target close date is August 31, 2026.
- The planned runway period is September 1, 2026 through August 31, 2028.
- The financing objective is to convert a substantial engineered corpus into customer-verifiable platform proof.
- The financing objective is not to fund indefinite research.
- The financing objective is not to claim production maturity before gates validate it.
- The financing objective is to prove FD-001 wedge readiness, policy enforcement, audit-chain evidence, pilots, and regulated deployment posture.
- The round should support enterprise design partners before broad go-to-market hiring.
- The round should support security and compliance evidence before regulated-market promises.
- The round should support pricing evidence before aggressive quota scaling.
- The round should support investor trust by tying use of funds to named milestones.
- The operating plan assumes a base monthly burn of $450,000 by Q4 2026.
- The operating plan assumes peak monthly burn of $850,000 by Q2 2028.

- The plan targets $1.5 million pilot ARR by December 31, 2027.

- The plan targets two paid enterprise pilots by March 31, 2027.
