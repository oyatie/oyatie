# Tenant Isolation Policy: Quality Management

## A. Rule
Every QM command, query, event, replay cursor, dashboard query, and export carries tenant_id and principal_id. No Quality Management bounded context may infer tenant scope from hostname, session cookie, marketplace settlement reference, or source-system identifier alone.

## B. Isolation evidence
- inspection-plan: API, worker, catalog, SLO, dashboard, Cedar, and audit evidence require tenant_id equality before mutation or disclosure.
- inspection-lot: API, worker, catalog, SLO, dashboard, Cedar, and audit evidence require tenant_id equality before mutation or disclosure.
- certificate-of-analysis: API, worker, catalog, SLO, dashboard, Cedar, and audit evidence require tenant_id equality before mutation or disclosure.
- quality-notification: API, worker, catalog, SLO, dashboard, Cedar, and audit evidence require tenant_id equality before mutation or disclosure.
- quality-hold: API, worker, catalog, SLO, dashboard, Cedar, and audit evidence require tenant_id equality before mutation or disclosure.
- audit-evidence: API, worker, catalog, SLO, dashboard, Cedar, and audit evidence require tenant_id equality before mutation or disclosure.

## C. Marketplace boundary
Marketplace settles tenant deals per ADR-0314. Quality Management stores settlement references only after tenant isolation has already been proven by policy, audit, and source-system checks.
