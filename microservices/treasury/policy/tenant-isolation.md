# Tenant Isolation Policy: Treasury

## A. Rule
Every TRM command, query, event, replay cursor, dashboard query, and export carries tenant_id and principal_id. No Treasury bounded context may infer tenant scope from hostname, session cookie, marketplace settlement reference, or source-system identifier alone.

## B. Isolation evidence
- cash-position: API, worker, catalog, SLO, dashboard, Cedar, and audit evidence require tenant_id equality before mutation or disclosure.
- liquidity-forecast: API, worker, catalog, SLO, dashboard, Cedar, and audit evidence require tenant_id equality before mutation or disclosure.
- bank-account: API, worker, catalog, SLO, dashboard, Cedar, and audit evidence require tenant_id equality before mutation or disclosure.
- debt-instrument: API, worker, catalog, SLO, dashboard, Cedar, and audit evidence require tenant_id equality before mutation or disclosure.
- fx-exposure: API, worker, catalog, SLO, dashboard, Cedar, and audit evidence require tenant_id equality before mutation or disclosure.
- hedge-designation: API, worker, catalog, SLO, dashboard, Cedar, and audit evidence require tenant_id equality before mutation or disclosure.

## C. Marketplace boundary
Marketplace settles tenant deals per ADR-0314. Treasury stores settlement references only after tenant isolation has already been proven by policy, audit, and source-system checks.
