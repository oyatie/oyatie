# Data Residency Policy: Real Estate

## A. Rule
Every record carries tenant_id, jurisdiction_code, residency_pack, retention_class, and audit_event_class. Cross-region copies require an explicit residency decision.

## B. Pack overlays
- SOX-404: activated only through tenant pack metadata and policy evidence.
- SOC-2: activated only through tenant pack metadata and policy evidence.
- ISO-27001: activated only through tenant pack metadata and policy evidence.
- GDPR: activated only through tenant pack metadata and policy evidence.
- LGPD: activated only through tenant pack metadata and policy evidence.
- KR-PIPA: activated only through tenant pack metadata and policy evidence.
- jurisdictional-tax: activated only through tenant pack metadata and policy evidence.

## C. Region handling
No regulated RE-FX record crosses a forbidden boundary. Replay refuses if the destination cell cannot prove residency eligibility.
