# Capacity Model: Plant Maintenance

## A. Method
Capacity is modeled with Little's Law: L = lambda * W. L is in-flight work, lambda is arrival rate, and W is average residence time. The service budgets command, query, worker, and replay paths separately because PM workloads burst differently by bounded context.

## B. Deployment Context and Tenant Class Assumptions
| deployment_context | tenant_class | Arrival rate | p99 target | In-flight budget |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| demo_trial_context | demo_trial | 25 rps | 400 ms | 10 |
| paid_standard_context | paid | 250 rps | 300 ms | 75 |
| paid_enterprise_context | paid | 2500 rps | 250 ms | 625 |
| paid_regulated_context | paid | 1500 rps | 300 ms | 450 |

## C. Hot partitions
The partition key is tenant_id plus bounded_context plus fiscal_or_operational_period. No source-system import may partition only by tenant_id. Worker replay leases use tenant_id plus source_system_id plus checksum_bucket.
