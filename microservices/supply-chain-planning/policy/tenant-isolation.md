# Tenant Isolation Policy: Supply Chain Planning

## A. Rule
Every SCM/APO command, query, event, replay cursor, dashboard query, and export carries tenant_id and principal_id. No Supply Chain Planning bounded context may infer tenant scope from hostname, session cookie, marketplace settlement reference, or source-system identifier alone.

## B. Isolation evidence
- demand-plan: API, worker, catalog, SLO, dashboard, Cedar, and audit evidence require tenant_id equality before mutation or disclosure.
- supply-network-plan: API, worker, catalog, SLO, dashboard, Cedar, and audit evidence require tenant_id equality before mutation or disclosure.
- available-to-promise: API, worker, catalog, SLO, dashboard, Cedar, and audit evidence require tenant_id equality before mutation or disclosure.
- replenishment-plan: API, worker, catalog, SLO, dashboard, Cedar, and audit evidence require tenant_id equality before mutation or disclosure.
- transportation-plan: API, worker, catalog, SLO, dashboard, Cedar, and audit evidence require tenant_id equality before mutation or disclosure.
- planning-scenario: API, worker, catalog, SLO, dashboard, Cedar, and audit evidence require tenant_id equality before mutation or disclosure.

## C. Marketplace boundary
Marketplace settles tenant deals per ADR-0314. Supply Chain Planning stores settlement references only after tenant isolation has already been proven by policy, audit, and source-system checks.
