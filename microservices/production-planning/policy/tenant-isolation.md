# Tenant Isolation Policy: Production Planning

## A. Rule
Every PP command, query, event, replay cursor, dashboard query, and export carries tenant_id and principal_id. No Production Planning bounded context may infer tenant scope from hostname, session cookie, marketplace settlement reference, or source-system identifier alone.

## B. Isolation evidence
- bom-revision: API, worker, catalog, SLO, dashboard, Cedar, and audit evidence require tenant_id equality before mutation or disclosure.
- mrp-run: API, worker, catalog, SLO, dashboard, Cedar, and audit evidence require tenant_id equality before mutation or disclosure.
- capacity-calendar: API, worker, catalog, SLO, dashboard, Cedar, and audit evidence require tenant_id equality before mutation or disclosure.
- routing-step: API, worker, catalog, SLO, dashboard, Cedar, and audit evidence require tenant_id equality before mutation or disclosure.
- production-order: API, worker, catalog, SLO, dashboard, Cedar, and audit evidence require tenant_id equality before mutation or disclosure.
- shop-floor-release: API, worker, catalog, SLO, dashboard, Cedar, and audit evidence require tenant_id equality before mutation or disclosure.

## C. Marketplace boundary
Marketplace settles tenant deals per ADR-0314. Production Planning stores settlement references only after tenant isolation has already been proven by policy, audit, and source-system checks.
