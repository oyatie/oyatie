## Summary
ADR-0612 governs **tier 3 (Remote Execution)** of the owned build-cache substrate — the follow-on to ADR-0560's tier-1 CAS. It stands up the two reserved K8s tiers (NativeLink scheduler + worker), flips `remote_enabled=true` for warm-eligible classes behind fail-closed per-identity authz, and lands the divergent-key eviction reconciler ADR-0560 deferred. Six crisp decisions, each carrying the hyperscaler precedent it reimplements and its trade-off:

- **D1 — Deploy scheduler + worker tiers.** Two manifests beside the existing CAS manifest, one pinned NativeLink v1.4.0 image, role-by-config. *Precedent:* Buildbarn `bb-scheduler`/`bb-worker`/`bb-storage` split + Google Bazel RBE (scheduler off the cache critical path); scale-to-zero aarch64 worker pool = EngFlow/BuildBuddy worker-farm.
- **D2 — RE execution platform + opt-in overlay, dark until licensed.** `toolchains//remote-exec:re-platform` (`remote_enabled=true`, `local_enabled=true` fallback) + one checked-in `remote-exec.buckconfig`; resolver adds an RE-eligibility axis (RE-eligible ⊆ warm-eligible — RE never widens the warm set). *Precedent:* Bazel `--remote_executor` + platform `exec_properties`; `--remote_local_fallback`.
- **D3 — Per-identity RE authz, fail-closed, as a bring-up prerequisite (not a follow-up).** Every RE leg presents an X.509-SVID (SPIFFE, ADR-0561); `re:execute`/`re:write-ac` is a fail-closed Cedar PDP call (ADR-0559), replacing tier-1's coarse two-CA split. *Precedent:* SPIFFE/SPIRE workload identity + AVP/Zanzibar externalized authz; RE Execute is remote code execution — the new-gRPC-surface default-deny doctrine binds hardest here.
- **D4 — Divergent-key eviction reconciler**, promoted from ADR-0556 D4.3 placeholder to a real, idempotent, receipt-emitting reconcile action driven off the canary-RED verdict (no shell, no operator command). *Precedent:* BuildBarn CAS-GC / completeness-checking as a level-triggered Kubernetes-style reconcile.
- **D5 — The canary is extended to cover RE'd outputs and gates the flip.** Byte-compares cold-from-source against RE-produced warm entries; pinned worker-image digest becomes a canary input; no GREEN RE-covering canary → no RE. *Precedent:* reproducible-builds bit-identity + Bazel remote-cache correctness; the "buck2 cache cold-vs-warm" doctrine made mechanical.
- **D6 — The four cold-required classes stay local/cold** (release-image, integrity-canary, untrusted-author-presubmit, provenance-attestation), conformance-pinned to bypass even under a licensed RE fixture. RE does **not** open ADR-0556 D1's one-way door. *Precedent:* SLSA hermetic-trusted-builder / Google "build for cache" vs "build for release provenance" separation.

Also carried: an explicit **Owned-stack destination** section (NativeLink is a transient adapter behind the owned **REAPI gRPC port**; the adopt-vs-reimplement destination is deferred to the FSL→Apache conversion window, non-blocking; the one server-specific exception — the D4 eviction reconciler's store-admin surface, since REAPI v2 has no delete/evict RPC — is isolated adapter-side), the per-decision precedents above, and the FSL-1.1 Tier-3 license posture recorded consciously (the ADR-0560 characterization is already consistent on `dev` per PR #1215 — nothing queued). The six one-way/sequencing questions (OQ1–OQ6) are **ratified** under the founder's 2026-07-08 autonomous-drive delegation after independent adversarial review found all six safe to ratify (each preserves its one-way door; this ADR is itself two-way) — decided, not deferred.
