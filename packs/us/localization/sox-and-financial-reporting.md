# SOX and Financial Reporting

This document maps Sarbanes-Oxley duties into US-PACK-1.
The document focuses on Sections 302, 404, 802, and 906.
The document treats ICFR as a scoped evidence system, not a generic security checklist.
The document treats PCAOB AS 2201 as the audit-control interpretation anchor for integrated audits.
The document does not modify accounting services in this slice.
The document does not author Cedar files in this slice.
The document establishes policy, model, API, audit, and failure-mode deltas for future implementation.
The document keeps SOX distinct from GLBA, FCRA, ECOA, SOC 2, NIST 800-53, and FedRAMP.
The document allows crosswalks to SOC 2, NIST 800-53, and FedRAMP only where evidence reuse is explicit.
The document requires issuer counsel, audit-firm, and finance-owner review before production certification reliance.

- ICFR-001: ICFR scope starts with issuer, reporting period, and report type.
- ICFR-002: ICFR scope identifies significant accounts.
- ICFR-003: ICFR scope identifies significant disclosures.
- ICFR-004: ICFR scope maps relevant assertions to each account and disclosure.
- ICFR-005: ICFR scope maps likely sources of material misstatement.
- ICFR-006: ICFR scope identifies entity-level controls.
- ICFR-007: ICFR scope identifies process-level controls.
- ICFR-008: ICFR scope identifies information-technology general controls.
- ICFR-009: ICFR scope identifies application controls.
- ICFR-010: ICFR scope identifies spreadsheets and end-user computing artifacts.
- ICFR-011: ICFR scope identifies third-party service organization dependencies.
- ICFR-012: ICFR scope identifies SOC 1 reports where service organizations affect ICFR.
- ICFR-013: ICFR scope may use SOC 2 evidence for security availability privacy controls only when linked to ICFR risk.
- ICFR-014: ICFR scope may use NIST 800-53 evidence for access, audit, configuration, change, and contingency controls only when linked to ICFR risk.

## PCAOB AS 2201 Control Interpretation

- AS2201-001: Use top-down risk selection rather than exhaustive low-risk control testing.
- AS2201-002: Start with financial statements and disclosures.
- AS2201-003: Identify entity-level controls that affect ICFR conclusions.
- AS2201-004: Evaluate controls over management override.
- AS2201-005: Evaluate controls over period-end financial reporting.
- AS2201-006: Identify significant accounts and disclosures.
- AS2201-007: Identify relevant assertions.
- AS2201-008: Identify likely sources of misstatement.
- AS2201-009: Select controls that address likely sources of misstatement.
- AS2201-010: Test design effectiveness before operating effectiveness.
- AS2201-011: Test operating effectiveness using evidence appropriate to risk.
- AS2201-012: Evaluate control deficiency likelihood.
- AS2201-013: Evaluate control deficiency magnitude.
- AS2201-014: Aggregate related deficiencies.
- AS2201-015: Identify material weaknesses.
- AS2201-016: Identify significant deficiencies.
- AS2201-017: Preserve auditor communication evidence.
- AS2201-018: Preserve audit committee communication evidence.
- AS2201-019: Preserve walkthrough evidence.
- AS2201-020: Preserve inquiry, observation, inspection, and reperformance evidence.
- AS2201-021: Preserve competence and objectivity evidence for work of others.
- AS2201-022: Preserve service-organization report evaluation.
- AS2201-023: Preserve complementary user entity control mapping.
- AS2201-024: Preserve IT dependency mapping.
- AS2201-025: Preserve automated control logic evidence.
- AS2201-026: Preserve change-management evidence for automated controls.
- AS2201-027: Preserve access-control evidence for financial systems.
- AS2201-028: Preserve privileged-access approval evidence.
- AS2201-029: Preserve segregation-of-duties evidence.
- AS2201-030: Preserve exception and remediation evidence.
- AS2201-031: Do not let a control owner self-approve evidence where independence is required.
- AS2201-032: Do not use stale SOC reports without bridge-letter evaluation where needed.
- AS2201-033: Do not use unscoped cloud control evidence as ICFR evidence.
- AS2201-034: Do not omit spreadsheet controls from financially significant workflows.
- AS2201-035: Do not collapse disclosure controls into ICFR without mapping.
- AS2201-036: Do not collapse ICFR into cybersecurity controls without financial-reporting relevance.
- AS2201-037: Do not issue clean status while material weakness remains unresolved.
- AS2201-038: Do not certify before late changes are evaluated.
- AS2201-039: Do not destroy workpapers or evidence before retention expires.
- AS2201-040: Do not export auditor evidence without scoped authorization.
