# HIPAA PHI Handling

This document is the US-PACK-1 operating contract for protected health information.
The document expands the federal privacy overview into PHI-specific routing, classification, access, disclosure, minimization, business-associate, de-identification, security, breach, and transaction-code-set handling.
The document covers 45 CFR Parts 160, 162, and 164.
The document does not create legal advice.
The document does not modify services in this slice.
The document does not author Cedar policies in executable form in this slice.
The document provides enough specificity for later schema, API, Cedar, and audit-chain implementation.
The document assumes healthcare tenants may be covered entities, business associates, subcontractor business associates, or non-HIPAA health-adjacent services.
The document requires counsel review before production use.
The document requires HHS OCR guidance refresh before any runtime enforcement.

## Minimum-Necessary Standard

- MIN-NEC-001: Require purpose classification before PHI access.
- MIN-NEC-002: Determine whether minimum necessary applies.
- MIN-NEC-003: If treatment disclosure to provider, record exception.
- MIN-NEC-004: If disclosure to individual, record exception.
- MIN-NEC-005: If valid authorization, record exception and authorization id.
- MIN-NEC-006: If HIPAA administrative simplification compliance, record exception.
- MIN-NEC-007: If HHS enforcement request, record exception.
- MIN-NEC-008: If required by law, record exception and legal basis.
- MIN-NEC-009: If no exception, require role-based minimum necessary profile.
- MIN-NEC-010: For routine workforce access, map job function to permitted PHI fields.
- MIN-NEC-011: For non-routine access, require case-by-case review.
- MIN-NEC-012: For analytics access, require dataset minimization before query execution.
- MIN-NEC-013: For support access, require ticket, patient-scope, and time box.
- MIN-NEC-014: For audit access, require evidence purpose and redaction plan.
- MIN-NEC-015: For payment access, require claim, eligibility, remittance, or billing purpose.
- MIN-NEC-016: For operations access, require quality, care coordination, utilization, compliance, training, or fraud-waste-abuse purpose.
- MIN-NEC-017: For research access, require waiver, authorization, preparatory-to-research, decedent-research, or limited-data-set basis.
- MIN-NEC-018: For public-health access, require recipient authority and scope.
- MIN-NEC-019: For law-enforcement access, require specific 164.512(f) basis.
- MIN-NEC-020: For judicial access, require court order, subpoena, qualified protective order, or satisfactory assurance metadata.
- MIN-NEC-021: Deny wildcard PHI export when purpose can be satisfied by limited fields.
- MIN-NEC-022: Deny bulk access when per-subject access satisfies the purpose.
- MIN-NEC-023: Deny persistent access when time-boxed access satisfies the purpose.
- MIN-NEC-024: Deny direct identifiers when de-identified or limited data set satisfies the purpose.
- MIN-NEC-025: Emit audit event for every minimum-necessary exception.
- MIN-NEC-026: Emit audit event for every minimum-necessary denial.
- MIN-NEC-027: Preserve reviewer id for non-routine access approval.
- MIN-NEC-028: Preserve data-field list for approved access.
- MIN-NEC-029: Preserve purpose statement for approved access.
- MIN-NEC-030: Preserve downstream recipient for approved disclosure.
- MIN-NEC-031: Preserve requestor role for approved access.
- MIN-NEC-032: Preserve time-to-live for support and emergency access.
- MIN-NEC-033: Preserve break-glass reason where emergency access is allowed.
- MIN-NEC-034: Require post-access review for break-glass access.
- MIN-NEC-035: Require periodic review of routine role profiles.
- MIN-NEC-036: Require revocation when job function changes.
- MIN-NEC-037: Require masking in logs, traces, and support exports.
- MIN-NEC-038: Require PHI-safe search indexes.
- MIN-NEC-039: Require data-loss-prevention checks for outbound exports.
- MIN-NEC-040: Require evidence retention for counsel and OCR review.
